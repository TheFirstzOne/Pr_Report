# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
- **Primary User**: Head of Procurement / Maintenance Department (simulated as "xxxx").
- **Context**: Working in an industrial or office environment managing spare parts inventory, supplier negotiations, and budget allocations.
- **Job to be Done**: Maintain safe inventory levels of spare parts, request pricing from suppliers, track and approve quotations, issue POs, and verify that spending aligns with the project budgets.

## Product Purpose
- Provide a unified, single-screen dashboard to manage the entire Procure-to-Pay (P2P) lifecycle for maintenance spare parts.
- Success looks like zero downtime due to stockouts, optimal budget utilisation, and clean audit logs matching real physical inventory.

## Positioning
- A high-density, high-efficiency dashboard linking real-time stock levels directly to procurement actions and monthly budget tracking.

## Brand Personality
- **Voice**: Professional, administrative, precise.
- **Tone**: Trustworthy, efficient, clear.
- **3-Word Personality**: Technical, Minimalist, Reliable.
- **Emotional Goals**: Sense of control, confidence in data, relief from administrative friction.

## Anti-references
- Saturated AI SaaS templates with beige backgrounds, decorative glassmorphism, or non-functional mock parameters.
- Text overflow on medium viewports or low-contrast text that hinders readability.

## Design Principles
- **Direct Affordance**: Actions (like issuing RFQs, approving quotes, and receiving POs) should be inline and require minimal navigational steps.
- **Realism First**: Use actual MAT codes, descriptions, and exact currency representations to match real-world operations.
- **High Information Density**: Present complete tables rather than hiding details inside nested tabs or generic card layouts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratios of at least 4.5:1 for body and placeholder text to ensure legibility under ambient factory floor lighting.
- Clear font scaling using Inter and Sarabun for readability in both Thai and English.
